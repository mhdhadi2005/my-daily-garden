# My Almond Forest: v2 experience design

Confirmed 16 October 2026 after the design interview. This is the agreed experience to prototype. [Product scoring and pilot decisions](v2-product-decisions.md) and the [working 10almonds brand kit](v2-brand-kit.md) remain the sources for those subjects. Unlock timing is provisional until the historical-award dry run.

## First view and page structure

- The fixed page title is **My Almond Forest**. There is no changing level title, personal forest name, or permanent subtitle. Keep copy short, controls obvious, and type large enough for older readers.
- The forest is the focal point. Its scene should blend with the site-wide warm background, without the current grey 3D backdrop. Use the 10almonds colours, type, logo, and playful illustration language.
- After the forest: show progress and points remaining to the **next named reward**, then six large stats in two rows. Desktop uses three columns; phones use two. The stats are current reported click streak, garden points, completed trees, email opens, reported newsletter clicks, and best reported click streak. Show streaks only if Beehiiv reporting proves reliable.
- Remove the latest-milestone block from the page. Keep the latest earned milestone on the fixed share image, along with the forest view, **My Almond Forest**, subscriber-since year, opens, reported clicks, and trees. No personal name, subscriber identifier, private link, or reported-click-day count.
- Put link recovery on the access screen. A clear Help and access link can sit near the bottom of the garden.

## Living forest

- Before Tree 1, show a planted seed in a starter patch and progress to the first tree. Trees visibly develop buds, blossoms, and almonds between completion milestones.
- On a return visit with new growth, start from the last-seen state and animate the points filling the meter and new trees or rewards appearing **in the forest**. Keep the replay around 2–3 seconds, skippable, and without a separate splash screen. With no change, let the current forest come into view. Provide a still summary for reduced-motion readers.
- Celebrate a new tree or reward with a brief blossom-and-growth effect. A newly unlocked choice follows the effect and **cannot be skipped**. If several choices accrued, present them together on one screen with two or three large pictured options each. Ordinary trees use the reader's current choices without interrupting the visit.
- On a first visit with historical activity, reveal the established forest once. For an established reader, require only one starter resident choice; use a default blossom palette that can be changed later. Do not replay past opens one by one.
- Selecting a tree visibly focuses it and opens a simple detail view: tree number, earned date, current look, and **Design this tree**. No invented tree names. A tree's earned identity persists when its look or placement changes.
- Connected Meadow, Pond, and Woodland areas use a simple area selector. Distant earned trees can form a backdrop, while every tree stays reachable through the selector and collection view. A resident has a gentle idle animation and small tap reaction.
- If 3D performs poorly or cannot run, show a clear still forest while keeping progress, stats, choices, and the tree collection usable.

## Reader actions

- **Design Your Garden** opens a dedicated mode. At launch, readers may move earned trees and decorations to valid spots, choose a blossom palette and resident, and undo changes. New items initially appear automatically. Tree family, rotation, terrain, and paths are not editing controls; areas and paths grow automatically. Show owned items and a few upcoming locked rewards with how to earn them.
- **How Your Garden Grows** is a full, visible section. Use short, readable rows to explain actual earning methods and point values, show the next unlock and future reward path, and link to a real quiz, referral page, or store when available. No dead actions or miniature afterthought disclosure.
- Keep the main view simple while allowing the reward catalogue to expand later. Launch with the agreed trees, palettes, residents, areas, decorations, growth replay, and reward choices. Add other game systems after observing reader use; no random drops or second currency at launch.

## Provisional reward path

Test the first tree at 60 cumulative garden points and each later tree after 250 more. Every completed tree is permanent. The first six trees introduce six different almond-tree families automatically; later trees reuse them with small natural variations.

| Tree earned | Added reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Meadow and a blossom-palette choice |
| 2 | Decoration |
| 3 | Choice of resident |
| 4 | Pond area |
| 5 | Another decoration |
| 6 | Woodland area |

After Tree 6, every third new tree unlocks a choice from a growing decoration collection. Rewards are guaranteed at their milestones. Tune the timing and starting unlocks against the historical-award dry run before launch.
