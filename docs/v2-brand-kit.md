# 10almonds brand kit for My Daily Garden

Observed on the live [10almonds homepage](https://10almonds.com/) on 16 October 2026. This is a working kit derived from the site, not an official brand manual.

| Element | Observed on site | Garden use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo | Black “10 almonds” wordmark, illustrated orange almonds, white sticker outline. [Original asset](https://10almonds.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Shadowd-Sticker-e1704952270842.png). | Use the actual mark in the masthead and share image. |
| Main colours | Theme variables: orange `#ffa01e`, navy `#262a45`, black `#0c0c0c`, slate `#4A5568`, muted `#5c6773`, pale blue `#EDF2F7`, near white `#F7FAFC`. | Navy for structure and scores, orange for the primary action and growth, pale background for breathing room. |
| Background | [ExtendedBackground.png](https://10almonds.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ExtendedBackground.png) adds a soft warm wash behind the homepage. | Reuse the asset in the prototype rather than inventing a new forest wallpaper. |
| Display type | Bowlby One SC, uppercase navy, used for the hero and section headings. | One large grove name and restrained section headings. |
| Supporting type | EB Garamond for the warm hero explanation; Rubik for body text and information. The main site CTA uses Poppins. | EB Garamond for one short invitation; Rubik for controls and stats. |
| CTA | The homepage's orange `#ffa01e` button is rectangular with a small 4px radius and no shadow. | One orange share action; other controls are plain text buttons. |
| Art | Playful thick outlined, white edged sticker illustrations. [Example](https://10almonds.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Comparison-1024x1024.png). | Treat the 3D forest as the hero and use one bold outline around its stage. Final almond-tree art should be judged against this illustration language. |
| Voice | “Age gracefully” and “100% free (and fun!) daily emails” are direct and upbeat. | Short, friendly game copy without ornamental labels. |

The prototype contains local copies of the public logo, background, and three fonts. Its garden trees remain provisional 3D geometry. The brand kit describes the current website; it does not establish approval for final illustration, typography licensing outside this project, or a permanent brand standard.
