# My Daily Garden

The 10almonds reader's earned growth and personal woodland.

## Language

**Garden points**:
Cumulative growth earned from credited actions. Completing a tree does not spend or reset these points.
_Avoid_: Balance

**Completed tree**:
A permanently earned almond tree in a reader's forest. Its identity and chosen appearance remain as the forest grows.
_Avoid_: Harvest

**Open credit**:
Garden points awarded for a reported newsletter open, at most once per reader and issue. An open report does not establish that the reader read the issue.

**Reported newsletter click**:
A link click included in Beehiiv's cumulative subscriber click total. It may be a repeat or a non-article link, and does not prove that the reader read an article.
_Avoid_: Article read

**Newsletter click credit**:
Garden points awarded when a reader's reported newsletter click total increases, subject to the earning cap.
_Avoid_: Article click credit

**Reported click day**:
A UTC day on which an increase in a reader's reported newsletter click total is observed. It may differ from the day the click happened.

**Reported click streak**:
A run of successful daily reports that each show new clicks. A failed report pauses the run, and a break never takes away earned garden points, trees, or items.
_Avoid_: Reading streak

**Pilot click outcome**:
Beehiiv's verified total email link clicks divided by delivered emails during the pilot. Every link counts, including the garden link, so this measures broad email interaction rather than article reading.
_Avoid_: Article-click rate

**Quiz credit**:
Garden points for a correct answer to an available, approved quiz, at most once per reader and quiz.

**Referral credit**:
Garden points for a unique referred subscriber confirmed by the newsletter referral record, at most once per referral.

**Purchase credit**:
Garden points tied to paid merchandise in one verified completed order, awarded at most once per order. A later refund does not remove growth already earned.

**Historical recognition**:
Starting garden growth based on recorded newsletter opens and clicks from before the garden launched. It is labelled with the period the records cover and kept separate from later earning credits; past purchases and referrals are excluded.

**Garden placement**:
A reader-chosen valid location for an earned tree or decoration. Moving it later changes its location without changing what was earned.

**Garden share card**:
An image of the reader's chosen forest view with a fixed summary of their garden and newsletter milestones. It contains no private garden link or subscriber identifier.
